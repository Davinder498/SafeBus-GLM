**Contact:** info@safebus.kids

## 1. Your SafeBus account

Your school authority invites you to use SafeBus for its authorized school transportation activities. You may use only the account and functions assigned to you. You must keep your sign-in credentials secure and must not share your account with anyone else. Contact the school authority if your access, student links or driver assignments are incorrect.

These user terms do not make you responsible for the school authority's subscription charges or authorize you to sign the authority's Master Services Agreement or Data Processing Agreement.

## 2. Guardian use

Guardians may view the assigned bus only for students they are authorized to access. SafeBus tracks the bus, not a child or the guardian's phone. You must not share a private live bus view with unauthorized people, use access to monitor another family, or attempt to view records outside your authorized student links.

A bus location, estimate or pickup/drop-off entry may be delayed or incomplete. It does not guarantee a child's physical location, supervision or safe arrival. Follow the school authority's transportation and emergency instructions.

## 3. Driver use

Drivers may use SafeBus only for assigned buses and trips. Never operate or read the application while driving. Do not falsify trip events, alter assignments, manipulate GPS reporting or share device credentials.

When you start an authorized trip, the approved mobile application may collect precise phone location while the trip is active, including when the screen is locked or the app is in the background. Collection of new fixes stops when the trip ends; earlier offline updates may still sync when connection returns. The separate driver Privacy Notice describes this processing. Device permissions, employer BYOD policies and applicable legal requirements remain independent of accepting these Terms of Use.

## 4. Security and acceptable behaviour

Do not impersonate another user; bypass account permissions; attempt to access another school authority's, driver’s or family's data; scrape or redistribute private transportation records; interfere with the service; or conduct unauthorized security testing. Do not submit student home addresses, health details, custody narratives or unrelated sensitive data in support messages or free-text fields.

Report a lost device, compromised account, incorrect access or suspected misuse promptly to your school authority. SafeBus can assist through info@safebus.kids. Do not include passwords, authentication links or unnecessary student information in an email.

## 5. Privacy and notifications

Read the Privacy Notice for your role at safebus.kids/privacy. Your acknowledgment confirms you have been shown the notice; it is not blanket consent to optional notifications, device-location access or unrelated data use. Email/push notification choices and device permission prompts are controlled separately.

The school authority determines your eligibility and the permitted use of its transportation records. SafeBus provides the software and separately handles its own necessary account, security and support information.

## 6. Reliability and operational decisions

Live locations, estimated arrival times and notifications depend on phone connectivity, device permissions, mapping services and other conditions. Do not treat SafeBus as an emergency dispatch service or the sole source for deciding when a child is safe. The school authority remains responsible for transportation operations, safety procedures and communication with families.

## 7. Account suspension and changes

The school authority can change or end your authorized access. SafeBus may temporarily restrict a compromised or misused account when needed for security, coordinating with the authority where practical and consistent with operational safety. Material changes to these terms will be presented for review and renewed acceptance as appropriate. Previously accepted versions remain in the secure receipt history.

## 8. Questions

For student links, routes, assigned trips or school transportation operations, contact the school authority that invited you. For questions about SafeBus's own account/privacy practices or to report a software security issue, contact info@safebus.kids.

These participant terms are subordinate to applicable law, privacy rights and the school authority's executed SafeBus agreement. No provision is intended to waive non-excludable legal rights or create a separate school transportation contract with the guardian or driver.
