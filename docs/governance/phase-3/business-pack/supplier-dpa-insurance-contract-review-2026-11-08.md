### Decision standard for each enabled supplier

An acceptable supplier record names (in **restricted evidence storage**) the entity contracting with Sachman Gold Inc., exact agreement/DPA source and applicable effective version, account acceptance mechanism, actual enabled processing purposes and permitted data categories, primary and secondary countries (including support/log/CDN/backup), subcontractors, retention/deletion/incident/assistance terms, and the responsible SafeBus approval. Only a non-sensitive reference to that evidence belongs in the [supplier approval ledger](../supplier-approval-ledger-2026-11-06.json). A public DPA can be legally incorporated through account terms; **do not** demand a separate signature just because the document is called a DPA. Conversely, **do not** mark an account accepted or a vendor approved from the public page alone.

A supplier used only for business billing or internal CI is not automatically part of the district's transportation-data subprocessor appendix. Disclose the distinct company-business flow as applicable, while listing suppliers that genuinely process school-authority transport data and technical metadata. The user-selected privacy and email/push permission controls remain distinct.

## B. Alberta school-authority contract fit

[Alberta OIPC's POPA service-provider contracting guidance](https://oipc.ab.ca/resource/popa-guidance-service-providers/) confirms that school public bodies remain accountable for their information and must address custody/control, lawfully permitted processing, safeguards, subcontractors, access/correction cooperation, audit/oversight and PIA applicability in their service-provider contracts. The relevant service-provider status under POPA is **not** employment in a labor-law sense. Final district MSA/DPA must reflect that the authority controls its transportation information, SafeBus may act only on documented authorized instructions, and the district must make its own procurement, role and PIA decisions.

The existing standard online publishing system bundles **eight immutable document versions** and supports a tenant administrator's affirmative electronic acceptance with an authority attestation. It is *not* a substitute for the district's procurement signatory authority or for a customer-specific Order Form if the published standard terms cannot correctly describe its actual price, term, bus allowance and tax. The separately published driver/guardian Terms of Use version `2026.1` **is not** the district's automatically approved acceptable-use schedule.

**No school-authority catalog publication until** these are resolved in a coherent customer offering:

1. **Commercial:** final approved CAD per-bus prices; monthly/annual availability, trial, automatic charges vs invoicing/PO, service term, auto-renewal/cancellation and tax authority. The existing Stripe **CA$1.99 monthly and CA$20.29 annual** prices are owner-confirmed **temporary** and must **remain unchanged** until a later explicit instruction. They are **not** approved binding MSA/Order Form prices.
2. **Parties and authority:** corporate identity/trade name, formal business mailing/notice channel, signer evidence; district legal name, procurement/PIA status and authorized representative before district acceptance.
3. **Risk and coverage:** review insurer quotations and policy terms before selecting any general liability cap, privacy/security cap or stating insured coverage; coordinate indemnities and exclusions with actual available insurance.
4. **Service promises:** only evidence-supported SLA measurements and credits; support Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00 Alberta local time is selected, but backup/holiday/P1 coverage and any after-hours promise remain open. Do not invent 24/7 staffing, uptime or GPS guarantees.
5. **Data handling:** verified supplier/country matrix, data categories/instructions, incident notification, access/correction assistance deadlines, retention/holds, export/exit/backup limits, and the district's POPA/ATIA/PIA applicability review. Do not activate destructive retention merely from a draft clause.
6. **Publication mechanism:** verify that one standard eight-document offer can correctly apply to a specific district. If order forms require customer-specific licensed quantities, pricing or procurement terms, extend/review the issuance mechanism **before** publication; don't pretend the current global catalog is personalized.

**Suggested *quote comparison only***: ask each broker for **CGL CA$2m**, **Technology E&O CA$1m and CA$2m options**, and **Cyber/Privacy CA$1m and CA$2m options**, with separately identified premium, deductibles, retention, exclusions, aggregate limits, insured entity, coverage territory, claims-made retroactivity/tail, and procurement endorsements. These numbers are **not owner-selected policy limits or legal requirements**. Confirm that school transportation data for minors, inaccurate/late GPS/ETA, notification outages, BYOD driver workflows and any bodily-injury claim arising from alleged software error are disclosed for underwriting; ordinary CGL/E&O may exclude or sublimit these. Do not claim SafeBus itself transports children or operates a school-bus fleet.

Do not submit a binding insurance application, accept an offer, pay a premium or certify insured status without the business owner's express authorization and truthful underwriting answers. The absence of a sample quote is not a reason to email every vendor or to invent insurer pricing.
