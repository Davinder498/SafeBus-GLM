# SafeBus Data Processing Agreement — Draft

**Status:** Proposed schedule to the MSA; not executed
**Parties:** Sachman Gold Inc. (SafeBus) and `[CUSTOMER_LEGAL_NAME]` (Customer)

## 1. Roles and instructions

For the transportation information identified below, SafeBus performs the
agreed service on the Customer's documented lawful instructions. The Customer
retains its applicable public-body responsibilities. The parties confirm the
applicable POPA/ATIA role and statutory service-provider treatment before
activation. Generic controller/processor language does not replace Alberta's
statutory framework or authorize collection.

SafeBus's separately necessary business-account, billing, security and support
activities are described in the business privacy policy and require their own
purpose and applicable PIPA/PIPEDA assessment. They do not authorize secondary
use of the Customer's transport records. The Customer need not approve ownership
of SafeBus; it authorizes only its own data use and contractual instructions.

SafeBus alerts the Customer if an instruction appears unlawful or outside the
approved scope, pauses the disputed processing where appropriate and seeks
resolution. Compelled disclosures are limited and notified where law permits.

### 1.1 Alberta records and service-provider responsibilities

For this DPA, personal information has the meaning assigned by POPA. Information
SafeBus receives, creates or derives while providing the Customer's transport
service remains under the Customer's control even when SafeBus holds it. POPA
and ATIA continue to apply to those records as applicable. SafeBus complies with
its statutory service-provider/employee responsibilities for these activities;
this does not make its personnel employees of the Customer for labour purposes.
The Customer retains access-decision authority; SafeBus provides assistance and
does not acquire an independent right to use transportation records.

If SafeBus receives a demand outside the agreed disclosure instructions, including
from an authority outside Alberta, it immediately informs the Customer's privacy
contact unless prohibited by law, preserves relevant records and assesses the
request's validity and scope. It seeks written Customer direction before any
voluntary disclosure. A binding legal obligation is not defeated by a consent
requirement: SafeBus discloses only what the law requires, documents its basis,
seeks available protective measures and gives delayed notice when permitted.

### 2.1 Customer-specific instructions and notices

Before activation, the processing appendix identifies actual collected and
created record categories, permitted uses/disclosures and each activity's lawful
authority, direct or indirect source, recipients and required notices/consents.
SafeBus collects only necessary approved fields and displays the applicable
Customer-approved collection notice identifying its service-provider role and
privacy contact. New uses, derived information and additional recipients need
review before introduction. No generic permission to use every available field
is granted. The Customer remains responsible for establishing its lawful basis.

The parties assess any automated output against applicable notice requirements;
ordinary scheduling or ETA labels do not establish an exemption. No unreviewed
AI feature is authorized. SafeBus takes reasonable steps within its service to

## 3. Authorized access and confidentiality

Included setup assistance can involve Customer-directed processing when the
Customer authorizes help with its routes, stops/geometry, buses, student records,
guardian links, drivers or assignments. Guidance and walkthroughs do not by
themselves require source-data transfer or record access. Where access is needed,
use only minimum authorized records, named school-authority-scoped accounts,
existing permissions and administrator MFA. Record the instruction, work and
access review/removal. No cross-customer support entitlement is created.

The Customer verifies guardian authority and its operational arrangements.
If source files are necessary, agree a restricted transfer channel; ordinary
support email is not a roster-upload channel. Temporary copies require documented
purpose, approved retention/hold handling and verified disposal. Do not request
prohibited student identifiers, home addresses, health or custody narratives.

SafeBus limits personnel access by operational need, binds authorized personnel
to confidentiality duties, trains them and maintains reviewed access records.
Routine platform control-plane access does not include Customer students,
manifests or live locations. Any necessary exceptional investigation must use
a separately documented, authorized and audited process; the agreement itself
does not create a broad support browsing entitlement or an RLS bypass.

Customer accounts and linked access must be current. Guardian links are not
automatically re-established after removal. Revocation, expired links and open
realtime/native sessions require verified behavior, rather than relying on a
UI role label. The Security Schedule and acceptance evidence govern safeguards.

### 3.1 Personnel accountability

SafeBus requires written confidentiality obligations before personnel access
Customer information, maintains an up-to-date access roster and supplies relevant
personnel, agent and subcontractor access details securely to the Customer on
request. SafeBus remains responsible for the acts of those acting on its behalf
in performing this agreement; existing monetary limits and mandatory exceptions
remain governed by the MSA, rather than being silently replaced here.

Personnel handling these records receive role-appropriate privacy/security
instruction before access and refresh it annually. SafeBus maintains completion
dates, participants and current materials, supplies the Customer written training
evidence and relevant materials/updates, and verifies equivalent downstream
arrangements for authorized subcontractors. This obligation does not assert that
vendor training records have already been obtained or that a cloud supplier has
accepted bespoke terms. Supplier feasibility must be resolved before publication.

The owner, acting as SafeBus Privacy Lead, is accountable through info@safebus.kids.
The accepted Order Form records the Customer privacy officer/contact and the
verified urgent route. Personnel identities and coverage details are kept in
restricted records rather than the public contract repository.

SafeBus gives at least 30 calendar days' advance notice of a planned material

is permitted for urgent containment; it does not authorize an unapproved supplier
or country. Customer-specific and mandatory legal requirements remain controlling.

## 5. Requests and customer cooperation

For Customer records, the Customer normally decides statutory access,
correction and complaints; SafeBus promptly routes received requests and
provides secure retrieval, correction and disclosure assistance within the
agreed support window, allowing the Customer to meet legal deadlines.
SafeBus acknowledges ordinary Customer records requests within two business days
and provides an action plan within five business days of receipt. Business days
follow the SLA's America/Edmonton calendar, excluding its specified Alberta
holidays. Receipt outside staffed hours is treated as the next business day's
opening; both periods run from that receipt, not from acknowledgment. The action
plan identifies scope, responsible contact, secure handling and a completion date
agreed with the Customer. These are response commitments, not a five-day completion
guarantee. Earlier applicable statutory or accepted Customer deadlines take
precedence and require escalation; incidents follow section 6 immediately. Identity/representation is verified proportionately;
guardian bus visibility is not automatically authority for every student record.

SafeBus does not delay reporting a request until the Customer's response period
has expired. Own-business requests go directly to the SafeBus privacy contact.
Requests, outcomes and necessary holds are documented in restricted storage.
Regulatory cooperation and audit access are subject to lawful scope, protection
of other tenants, appropriate confidentiality and reasonable arrangements.

### 5.1 Complaints and reviews

SafeBus routes complaints about Customer-controlled records to the Customer's
privacy officer promptly, preserves relevant evidence and provides a documented
account of its investigation and corrective work. The Customer leads its access,
correction and complaint decisions and OIPC interactions; SafeBus cooperates with
reviews/investigations and complies with any duties applying directly to it.
Assistance covers responsive service records under ATIA, not just personal
information. SafeBus records requests, routing, searches, results and delivery
securely and keeps the Customer informed of impediments before deadlines expire.
No contract term limits a person's lawful complaint rights.

## 6. Incidents and assurance

A breach includes loss, unauthorized access or unauthorized disclosure of personal
information. SafeBus provides urgent privacy/security incident coverage 24 hours
a day, seven days a week, including weekends and holidays. On learning of a breach
affecting the Customer's information, SafeBus immediately notifies its verified
privacy contact, takes proportionate containment action and preserves evidence.
Personnel and subcontractors must escalate suspected incidents immediately.
Initial notice may be incomplete; provide updates with known scope, information
involved, risks, containment, contact and next steps. Do not await a final forensic
report, ordinary support hours or a 24-hour deadline. This urgent coverage does
not promise continuous detection, a fixed repair time or 24/7 ordinary support.
The owner approved this commitment on November 11, 2026; operational routing and
coverage verification remain prerequisites to publication, not proven facts.

The parties allocate Customer/public-body and SafeBus own-business regulator
and individual notifications according to applicable law; neither can contract
away its own legal obligation. Operational severity does not replace the legal
harm/reporting assessment. No universal 72-hour statutory rule is presumed.

SafeBus supplies relevant security evidence, assists the Customer's PIA and
reasonable compliance review, and reports material unresolved findings. No SOC 2,
ISO, insurance, hosted isolation or recovery certification is implied.

The proposed Provider monetary caps in MSA section 8, if later accepted by a
Customer, are separate from and do not eliminate or postpone any non-excludable
privacy/security duty, incident notification, cooperation, access assistance,
data return or deletion obligation. A privacy/security financial cap is not
insurance or permission to diminish safeguards under this DPA. No policy,
coverage amount or insurance certificate is represented. Any limited
third-party privacy/security indemnification in a later accepted MSA
applies only to covered claims attributable to SafeBus's own breach,
including acts for which it is contractually responsible. That indemnity
uses the agreed privacy/security monetary cap for reasonable defence costs
and damages, with no separate additive cap; it does not excuse the
Customer's independent unlawful instructions or its own legal duties.
This indemnity clause is **not** a substitute for the timely independent
incident notification, safeguards and cooperation duties stated above.

SafeBus provides compliance information relevant to the Customer's service and

The Security Schedule identifies accepted administrative, technical and physical

an independent audit or security certification.

## 7. Retention and exit

The owner-approved approach is **school-authority-controlled data management**:
authorized tenant administrators can download supported student, guardian,
driver and bus directory CSVs, and use existing record-level deletion, archive
and preservation controls only where the current application and applicable
law permit. A directory download is not a complete historical data export;
deleting an on-screen record does not erase unrelated trip history, supplier
logs, audit evidence, immutable backups or legally preserved information.

The accepted Data Management, Return and Destruction Schedule identifies
purpose-based retention, lawful holds, customer-directed requests, export
scope and supplier/backup handling. The Customer can request additional
securely scoped records or account closure through the verified support
channel, subject to authority and recipient checks. Scoped assisted return is
included in the subscription where supported directory downloads are insufficient.
Before activation, the accepted order records applicable return formats, secure
delivery, completion dates or scheduling rules, retained exceptions and disposal
triggers. Each assisted request receives the section 5 response commitments and
an agreed scoped completion date supported by the tested process. **No universal 30-day
return or 60-day deletion commitment** is implied by this standard model;
any customer-specific deadline requires a tested process and written
agreement. Destructive automated retention remains separately gated and
is not activated by contract approval.

Account disablement, unlinking, primary deletion, provider deletion and
backup expiry are distinct actions. Neither party should certify complete
erasure without evidence of actual scope and lawful exceptions. The
Customer's own statutory recordkeeping duties and SafeBus's separate
DPA/security/financial retention duties remain intact.
