# SafeBus Acceptable-Use Terms — Draft

**Status:** Proposed terms; effective version and acceptance process pending

Use SafeBus only for the participating authority's authorized transportation
activities and your assigned role. The school authority contracts for the paid
service; these user rules do not make guardians or drivers responsible for its
subscription fees. A guardian cannot accept a school contract for the authority.

Administrators maintain accurate assignments and verified links, promptly remove
unneeded access and use required MFA. Drivers operate only their authorized
trips and do not manipulate tracking or operate a phone unsafely while driving.
Guardians use only visibility provided through their active authorized links;
they do not share live-location information with unauthorized people.

Do not:

- Share accounts, impersonate people, bypass permissions/RLS or seek another
  tenant, school, driver or guardian's information.
- Upload prohibited student identifiers, home addresses, health data, custody
  narratives or unrelated educational information, including in support messages.
- Track a child, off-duty driver, unrelated device or location outside the
  permitted active-trip purpose.
- Scrape manifests/location data, publish private information, conduct unrelated
  profiling, advertising, resale or AI training using transport records.
- Falsify assignments/events, tamper with GPS/credentials, disrupt the service,
  upload malicious content or perform unauthorized security/load testing.
- Treat a stale map or recorded event as a guarantee of a person's whereabouts,
  an emergency dispatch service or a substitute for school safety procedures.

Report suspected unauthorized access or a lost device promptly through the
school's incident contact or `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]`/`[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`. Do not include
credentials or unnecessary sensitive records in a report. SafeBus may restrict
an affected account/path proportionately to address credible misuse and will
coordinate with the Customer where appropriate. Provide an investigation/appeal
contact and preserve evidence. Protective restrictions are separate from billing
delinquency and must consider active operational safety.

Customer instructions, privacy rights and applicable law still apply. Changes
require the agreed notice process. The owner approves final terms and acceptance
mechanics; this draft neither publishes them nor enables excluded features.
