# SafeBus Order Form / Commercial Terms

**Version:** 2026.11.13.1  
**Provider:** Sachman Gold Inc., operating as SafeBus  
**Provider notices and support:** info@safebus.kids

## 1. Your school authority order

This common commercial schedule and the **Your school authority order** details
appended by SafeBus together constitute the Customer-specific Order Form. The
school authority's administrator prepares those details before acceptance. They
identify the Customer and account, selected billing interval, contracted bus
quantity, Canadian-dollar unit price, tax-inclusive treatment, payment method,
privacy and urgent contacts, service geography, trial and activation rules,
assisted-return arrangements and accepted Supplier Disclosure fingerprint.

The administrator must verify that the Customer name identifies the contracting
school authority and that they have authority to bind it. The complete agreement
becomes effective at the acceptance time recorded in the receipt. The receipt
preserves the signer's identity and the exact eight document versions and order.
Saving an order is not acceptance, a payment or subscription activation. Changing
an accepted order requires a new accepted revision; the prior receipt is retained.

## 2. Price, quantity and tax

| Selected interval | Standard unit price | Standard term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | CA$1.99 per active bus per month, including applicable GST/HST | Month-to-month, automatically renewing monthly |
| Annual | CA$20.29 per active bus per year, including applicable GST/HST | 12 months, automatically renewing for successive 12-month terms |

The captured quantity is the contracted active-bus allowance. The standard
recurring price is that quantity multiplied by the selected unit price; applicable
GST/HST is included, not added above it. Inactive/reserve vehicles cannot be used
to exceed the active-bus allowance. Adding records does not silently amend the
accepted billing quantity. Changes to quantity, price, effective date, discounts
or proration require an accepted change consistent with the MSA. Any separately
agreed commercial exception must be explicit; these standard terms do not invent
one. Invoices state the applicable tax treatment. Guardians and drivers do not
owe the school authority's subscription fees.

## 3. Trial, collection and activation

An eligible first-time school authority receives one **14-day free trial**. It
starts only when the subscription is confirmed. Previously subscribed or trialed
authorities do not receive another trial. Preparing or accepting this agreement
does not itself start the trial or charge the Customer.

Standard automatic payment uses Stripe-hosted checkout to collect a payment
method. At confirmation, the Customer sees the actual trial end, first charge,
selected interval and amount, and renewal and cancellation consequences. Unless
canceled before the trial expires, recurring billing starts at trial end.

Invoice/PO collection requires a separate SafeBus approval of the billing
contact, bill-to address, interval, quantity, price, any PO and a selected due
window of **14, 30, 45 or 60 calendar days after invoice issuance**. The authority
then confirms activation. Approval alone creates neither an invoice nor a
subscription. An eligible first-time authority receives the same trial without
a card, with its first invoice issued at trial end. Otherwise, activation may
issue an invoice immediately. Renewal invoices continue until cancellation;
issued invoices remain payable. No blanket net-30 arrangement applies.

Service starts after agreement acceptance, confirmed billing activation and any
applicable agreed operational conditions. Actual trial, billing and renewal dates
appear in the billing confirmation. A separately approved exemption or pilot does
not authorize a charge or override its recorded scope.

## 4. Renewal, cancellation and remedies

The Customer may cancel renewal at any time, effective at the end of the current
trial or paid term, with **no early cancellation fee**. Cancel before trial expiry
to avoid the first subscription charge. There is **no partial refund** solely for
convenience cancellation, except as required by law or expressly agreed. Issued
invoices, material-breach remedies, mandatory rights and data-return duties remain.
Use the available billing cancellation controls or contact info@safebus.kids for
assistance. Canceling payment does not certify deletion of records.

## 5. Service, assistance and support

The service covers the authority's authorized Alberta school transportation
operations and active-bus visibility within its accepted scope and allowance.
School records are organizational metadata; the contracting account is the
school authority. Supported web and Android functions are subject to device,
network, permissions and the MSA's scope limits. iOS delivery is not included in
this version. A numerical allowance is not proof of tested simultaneous capacity;
controlled pilot limits and separately agreed expansion remain applicable.

Setup assistance with routes/maps, stops, buses, minimum student records,
guardians/links, drivers and assignments is included. Any access to Customer
records requires named, scoped authorization and existing security controls.
Additional services require separate scope and price agreement.

All staffed support, including critical incidents, is **Monday–Saturday,
6:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. America/Edmonton**, with **no holiday exclusions**. Contact
info@safebus.kids. No 24/7 staffing, fixed repair time, numerical uptime/GPS/ETA/
delivery/recovery guarantee or automatic SLA credits are offered. The DPA's
immediate breach notice upon actual awareness remains independent of staffed
hours. The Customer's supplied privacy and urgent contacts must remain current.

## 6. Privacy, suppliers and records

The Customer accepts the versioned DPA and Supplier Disclosure, including the
disclosed international processing and unverified secondary location limits.
Canada-only processing and an exhaustive country list are not promised. The
Customer establishes its lawful authority and required notices before processing
records. New material supplier/country changes follow affirmative approval under
the DPA; acceptance does not permit arbitrary purposes or waive applicable law.

Supported directory downloads and record-level controls remain available to
authorized administrators. Scoped assisted return beyond them is included:
request it through support; SafeBus acknowledges within two business days and
provides an action plan within five under the DPA. The parties agree the scope,
verified recipient, secure machine-readable format and a tested completion date.
Lawful holds, retained exceptions, disposal triggers and documented supplier expiry
are addressed per request. No blanket 30-day return, 60-day purge or immediate
backup deletion is promised. Destructive automatic disposal is not activated by
acceptance.

## 7. Liability, insurance and incorporated documents

MSA section 8 applies: the Provider's general aggregate cap is the greater of
**CA$25,000 or 12-Month Fees**; its privacy/security aggregate cap is the greater
of **CA$100,000 or twice 12-Month Fees**. The MSA defines fees, non-stacking,
agreement-wide aggregation without incident/renewal resets, preserved duties and
exceptions for fraud, wilful misconduct and legally non-excludable liability.
Its limited fault-based privacy/security and software-IP indemnities include
covered defence costs, settlements and damages within the applicable cap. No
reciprocal Customer indemnity is added. **No insurance coverage is represented.**

The complete package comprises this Customer-specific Order Form, MSA, DPA,
Security Schedule, SLA, School-Authority Acceptable Use Terms, Data Management,
Return and Destruction Schedule, and Supplier Disclosure. Seven documents are
common versions; only the Order Form contains the appended Customer-specific
values. The Provider's published offer needs no separate countersignature.
Any required procurement variation must be expressly resolved before acceptance.
