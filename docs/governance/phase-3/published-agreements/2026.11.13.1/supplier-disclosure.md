# SafeBus Supplier Disclosure

**Version:** 2026.11.13.1  
**Provider:** Sachman Gold Inc., operating as SafeBus  
**Privacy and supplier questions:** info@safebus.kids

This disclosure is incorporated in the accepted DPA and applies to the named
services and purposes below. It distinguishes transportation-service processing
from SafeBus's separate business processing. It is not a supplier certificate,
an insurance statement or a claim that every vendor has accepted bespoke terms.

## 1. International processing and location limits

**SafeBus uses the named suppliers internationally. Disclosed locations reflect
verified information; Canada-only processing and an exhaustive list of support,
CDN, log, backup or downstream processing countries are not guaranteed.** A Canadian
primary database does not keep email, hosting functions, maps, push messages or
supplier support entirely in Canada. Information processed abroad may be subject
to that jurisdiction's lawful access requirements.

The Customer's affirmative acceptance authorizes these disclosed international
flows for the DPA's permitted purposes, subject to applicable law. It does not
waive the Customer's public-body duties, authorize unrelated uses, or permit
prohibited information. If the Customer requires exact country restrictions or
Canada-only processing, resolve those requirements before affected use; this
standard package does not provide them.

## 4. Retention, supplier terms and evidence

Application deletion, provider-log deletion, email expiry, immutable-backup expiry
and recipient-managed copies are distinct. SafeBus does not promise a single
expiry period for all suppliers. Its Data Management, Return and Destruction
Schedule governs verified scoped requests and retained exceptions.

Resend's published standard email/log retention, backup handling and post-account-
termination deletion are different triggers, not a guarantee of immediate removal
of every copy. Geoapify distinguishes ordinary request logs from exceptional
security/fraud retention. Netlify's enterprise-specific retention material is not
assumed to apply to SafeBus's current plan. A planned paid-plan upgrade is not
completed coverage. SafeBus identifies the applicable verified periods and
remaining uncertainties when responding to a scoped return/deletion request.

Standard vendor data-protection terms and available assurance reports support
supplier oversight. They do not establish that a vendor accepted every SafeBus
contract obligation, guarantee access to its premises, or provide individualized
staff training records. Those rights/evidence are limited by actual downstream
terms. SafeBus supplies available relevant evidence securely, identifies missing
evidence and material limitations, and remains responsible for its own DPA duties.
It must resolve a material inability to meet a necessary safeguard before the
affected processing. A supplier's ISO or SOC report does not certify SafeBus.

## 5. Changes and Customer control

Planned material supplier additions or processing-country changes require at
least **30 calendar days' notice and recorded affirmative Customer approval**
before affected processing, as stated in DPA section 4. Silence and continued use
are not approval. SafeBus reviews supplier notices and newly verified material
country information. The disclosed international scope does not erase the change
obligation or authorize a new purpose.

Downstream providers may give shorter notice; that does not silently amend the
Customer agreement. SafeBus must restrict/stop the affected flow if necessary
approval or safeguards cannot be maintained and arrange a lawful alternative or
exit. Urgent containment does not authorize an unapproved new supplier. Existing
accepted processing can continue within its permitted scope.
