SafeBus helps your school authority operate transportation and show you the
bus assigned through your active authorized student link. It tracks the bus,
not your child, and does not require your own GPS for this view.

The authority supplies the minimum student name, grade, school, transport
assignment and verified guardian relationship. Your name, email, optional
contact details, account/session and notification choices support sign-in,
authorized visibility and enabled email. Drivers see only the minimum assigned
operational information; guardians do not receive a full student manifest or
raw trip-location history. Bus location and recorded events may still reveal
information about identifiable people and are treated accordingly.

The authority determines the applicable collection/disclosure authority and
notice: `[CUSTOMER_COLLECTION_USE_DISCLOSURE_AUTHORITY]`. SafeBus processes
transport records on its instructions and separately handles its necessary
business/security/support records as described in the business privacy policy.
Your guardian app link does not settle a statutory right to every student record.

Enabled pickup/drop-off emails use **generic event notices** with no student name, bus number, stop or event time in the external subject/body; full event details stay in the authorized SafeBus inbox. The Netlify published source was verified to include this change on November 8, 2026 (PR #414; [attestation run](https://github.com/Davinder498/SafeBus-GLM/actions/runs/42543503884)); a live synthetic Resend delivery test remains outstanding. Earlier delivered emails are not retroactively modified, and other email types can include necessary account or operational information. Resend processes recipients, generic event text and delivery logs in the United States. The school authority determines which external notifications and recipients are authorized. An email event is not continuous child tracking or proof of current whereabouts.
Android FCM push requires your opt-in and device permission. It sends the device token, an opaque notification identifier, event category and necessary delivery metadata to Google. Following the protected November 8 server update, the provider-visible notification **text** is generic and excludes student names, bus/route/stop labels and coordinates. Full details stay in the authorized SafeBus inbox. Student QR remains outside this
commercial notice pending its own acceptance. Maps disclose
viewport tile coordinates, IP and request metadata to the map provider. Hosting,
Auth and email providers process necessary information; actual countries and
retention must be supplied in the approved supplier disclosure before publication.
Do not promise all service processing stays in Canada from database region alone.

We exclude student home addresses, health information, custody narratives,
prohibited government student identifiers and child-device tracking. Do not send
such information in support requests. Transport records are not used for resale,
advertising, unrelated profiling or AI training.

Manage your own optional email and push choices through notification settings.
The authority cannot opt you in or override your device permission.
Request access/correction/deletion of transport information through the authority's
privacy office; contact SafeBus directly for its own business records, concerns
or routing help, even if you cannot sign in. Verification protects other people.
The accepted schedule must describe retention, lawful holds and backup expiry;
account closure does not necessarily delete all transport/financial/security
records. Insert approved periods and working https://safebus.kids/account-deletion before publication.

The final notice must identify applicable rights, contacts, safeguards, supplier
countries and complaint channels. SafeBus's complete business privacy policy will
be at https://safebus.kids/privacy. Material changes require appropriate notice and
review; this draft is not a published notice or approval of hosted security.
