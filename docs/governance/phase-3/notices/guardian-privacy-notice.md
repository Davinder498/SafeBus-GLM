# SafeBus Alberta — Guardian Privacy Notice (Draft)

**Status:** Draft for counsel approval — not legal advice
**Owner:** Privacy Lead
**Phase:** 3 — Alberta privacy and legal readiness
**Last updated:** 2026-08-07

---

## 1. Purpose

Phase 3 requires a **guardian privacy notice**. This is the engineering
draft of the notice text; counsel approves the final wording and the
customer publishes it to guardians through the agreed channel.

> Plain-language note for reviewers: this notice tells guardians what
> SafeBus collects about them and their student, why, who sees it, how long
> it is kept, and how to exercise their rights.

## 2. Who we are and what we do

SafeBus Alberta is a school transportation operations platform. We help
your school authority operate bus transportation and give you, the
guardian, narrow visibility of the **bus** assigned to your student's
active trip.

We follow one rule above all: **we track the bus, not the child.** We do
not track your student's phone, a wearable, or any personal GPS device.

## 3. What we collect

- **Your contact information** — name and email (and phone if your school
  authority provides it) so we can send you transportation notifications
  and so you can sign in.
- **Your link to your student** — your school authority tells us which
  student(s) you are authorized to view. We record the link and who set it
  up, **not** any custody or family-court information.
- **Your notification consent** — you can choose whether to receive
  pickup/drop-off notifications.
- **Your student's transportation information** — the minimum needed for
  routing and for you to see the assigned bus (name, grade, school,
  route/stop assignment). We do **not** collect the Alberta Student
  Number, home address, health data, or any custody information.

## 4. Why we use it (purposes)

- To operate the bus trip your student is assigned to.
- To show you the **bus** location during an active trip — only during
  that trip, and only the bus, not your student.
- To send you pickup/drop-off notifications **if** you have turned them on.
- To keep the service secure and accountable.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, resale, or any
unrelated purpose.

## 5. Who sees it

- Your school authority (the customer) and its transportation staff.
- The assigned driver — only the minimum information needed to operate the
  trip safely.
- Our subprocessors (hosting, authentication, email delivery) — under
  contract, and only as needed to run the service. A current subprocessor
  list is available on request and published externally.

We do not share your information with other guardians, other tenants,
advertisers, or anyone else except as required by law.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above and
as required by law. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it
according to our retention schedule. Specific retention periods are
confirmed with privacy counsel.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, and you can
ask questions about how it is handled. Start with your school authority;
if they cannot resolve it, contact us through the channel in your customer
agreement. We verify your identity before acting on a request.

## 8. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, strict access
controls (each user sees only what they are authorized to see), multi-factor
authentication for administrators, and a security audit trail. We have a
breach-response procedure and will notify affected individuals and the
appropriate authorities as required by law if a breach occurs.

## 9. Location of your information

Your information is processed and stored in Canada. We do not transfer it
cross-border except as approved by privacy counsel (for example, email
delivery in transit).

## 10. Changes to this notice

If we make material changes, we update this notice and inform you through
your school authority.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions, start with your school authority. If your matter is
not resolved, contact SafeBus through the channel in your customer
agreement.
