Start with the [owner decisions](business-pack/owner-decisions.md) and
[pack index](business-pack/README.md). The actual monthly and annual per-active-bus CAD catalog
is described in the [commercial billing alignment review](../commercial-billing-contract-alignment-2026-11-07.md). Owner-approved November 9 GST/HST-inclusive standard prices, trial and
renewal/cancellation rules are recorded in the commercial files. The pilot
staffed-hours/holiday and no-numerical-SLA-guarantee business choices are
also approved. Owner-approved **proposed** Provider liability caps
are greater of CA$25,000 or 12-Month Fees for general claims and greater
of CA$100,000 or twice 12-Month Fees for privacy/security claims, without
stacking or limiting non-excludable duties. Customer-specific acceptance,
tax reconciliation, indemnities, insurance procurement requirements (quotes
and purchase deferred), suppliers, DPA escalation and retention evidence
remain open. No insurance coverage is represented. Do not configure operational promises from unsupported claims.

Review roles and purpose/authority, allowed data, suppliers/countries, access/
correction support, incident duties, retention/holds, safeguards, audit and exit
as one consistent set. The DPA, PIA and notices must use the same accepted flows.
Verify actual safeguards and operator capability before representing them as
operational. No signatures or counsel approval are generated by this register.

Public-body customer-specific duties/signatures are completed at onboarding.
The business owner approves its own commercial choices, but cannot supply a
customer's statutory head/delegate sign-off by owning SafeBus. Legal review is
prudent and remains an internal Point 6 release gate; a general statutory
requirement to hire Alberta privacy counsel is not established by the reviewed
framework. Drafting is not contingent on that appointment.

Final changes require appropriate owner/customer review, versioned accepted
schedules and actual confidential evidence. Only non-secret approval/execution
references enter `privacy-readiness.json` after they pass. The launch gate remains
`not_approved`; no policy publication or commercial activation follows from this
register.
