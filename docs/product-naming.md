# SafeBus product name

Owner decision, 2026-10-15 (America/Edmonton): use **SafeBus** as the sole public
product name, with this exact capitalization. The operator and seller is
**Sachman Gold Inc., doing business as SafeBus**, as identified by the owner.
The owner remains the business Privacy Lead.

Use SafeBus in the website, mobile display name, store listing, notifications,
email sender display name, Stripe catalog/portal and current commercial/privacy
drafts. Alberta describes the service's operating context; it is not part of the
chosen product name. The tagline remains “Track the bus, not the child.”

Permanent technical identifiers remain unchanged: Android package and deep-link
scheme `com.safebusalberta.app`, workspace package names, `SAFEBUS_*` environment
variables, URLs, database project identity and immutable migration files. A
technical project or historical record may therefore retain an earlier name.
Known legacy platform notification copy is translated at the display/delivery
boundary; customer text and stored records are not rewritten.

The Stripe **SafeBus sandbox** already uses SafeBus and identifies Sachman Gold
Inc. in its product metadata and portal headline. This does not verify a live
Stripe legal profile or bank account. The website is unpublished, price remains
undecided, and the owner reports not being GST/HST registered. Contact email,
mailing address, registration evidence and signing authority remain pending.

Repository assets and drafts require human PR review and the normal release
process. Existing deployments, store submissions, configured sender-name
overrides and Supabase Auth email templates must be checked at release; no
external publication, DNS change or production change is performed by this PR.
Historical release evidence and privacy/readiness approvals are not rewritten
or granted by a name change.
